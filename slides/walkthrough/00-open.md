The ordinary shape of a study
===

Data. Cleaning. Exploring. Analysis. Writing it up.

Nothing here is new to you. What is new is that I do not type any of it —
I ask, in plain sentences, and watch what comes back.

<!-- end_slide -->

Two things to notice
===

**It works on the machine, not in a chat window.**

It opens the files, writes a script, runs it, reads the result. The script
stays on disk, so anyone can check what was actually done.

**Every answer comes with its workings.**

Watch how often it tells me something I did not ask for.

<!-- end_slide -->

Before we start
===

The data are **entirely synthetic** — simulated from the published summaries
of a phase 3 trial. No real patient is represented, and nothing here is
evidence about any drug.

No funding. No conflict of interest.
