What this was
===

**Five ordinary steps.** Data, cleaning, exploring, analysis, writing.

**No code typed by hand.** Only sentences.

**Everything left on disk.** The scripts, the tables, the figures — all of it
openable, all of it checkable by someone who was not in the room.

The skill is not the typing. It is knowing what to ask for, and how to check
what comes back.
