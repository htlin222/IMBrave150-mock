What you are about to watch
===

One terminal. One agent. Nothing typed by hand except the prompts.

We start with ten CSV files a hospital would actually hand you, and stop when
every number in a manuscript can be traced back to the file that produced it.

<!-- end_slide -->

Two things to watch, all the way through
===

**What I ask it**

The wording is the whole talk. Watch how much of the answer is decided
by the question.

**What it volunteers**

The useful part is rarely the number. It is what the agent says about the
number without being asked.

<!-- end_slide -->

Before we start
===

The data are **entirely synthetic** — simulated from the published summaries
of a phase 3 trial. No real patient is represented, and nothing here is
evidence about any drug.

No funding. No conflict of interest. No relationship with any AI vendor.
