# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

## What that means here

This repository is used to teach clinicians who have never opened a terminal.
A large part of its audience is, by design, at the beginning of something. So:

- **Beginner questions are the point, not an interruption.** "What is a
  hazard ratio" and "what is a shell" are both fair questions here.
- **Critique the analysis, not the analyst.** "This drops 560 patients
  silently" is useful. "This is amateur work" is not, and is usually less
  accurate.
- **Be specific about mistakes.** Say what is wrong, why it matters, and what
  it should be instead. Every trap documented in `live-demo/` got there because
  somebody made the mistake first.
- **No harassment**, in any form: personal attacks, demeaning comments about
  someone's background or level of expertise, unwelcome attention, or
  publishing others' private information.

## Clinical claims

This repository contains **only synthetic data**. Do not use issues, pull
requests or discussions here to make claims about atezolizumab, bevacizumab,
sorafenib, or the treatment of hepatocellular carcinoma. Nothing computed from
these files is evidence about any of them, and presenting it as if it were is
the one contribution that will be closed without discussion.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or contact the maintainer privately through the address on the
GitHub profile at <https://github.com/htlin222>. Reports are handled as
confidentially as the platform allows.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not follow this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or serious violations.
